CORE REQUIREMENT
The app must dynamically show exam countdowns, study plans, revision priorities, alerts, and relevant content based on:
- the learner’s chosen NSC subjects
- the official exam dates in the uploaded timetable
- the specific paper for each subject where applicable

2. LEARNER PROFILE LINKING
Each learner must have:
- grade
- exam year
- subject selection
- language level where relevant
- paper-specific schedule derived automatically from the timetable

If a learner selects:
- Mathematics
- Physical Sciences
- Life Sciences
- English HL
- Afrikaans FAL
- Geography
then the app must automatically build that learner’s personal exam calendar from the official timetable.

3. DYNAMIC CONTENT POPULATION
Populate content dynamically based on selected subjects and nearest exam dates.

For each learner, show:
- next exam
- days remaining to each exam
- revision priority by exam proximity
- subject-specific recommended study sessions
- paper-specific prep content
- exam sequence timeline
- “focus this week” recommendations
- daily revision tasks based on the closest exams

Rules:
- subjects with the nearest exam dates move to the top
- if Paper 1 is approaching, prioritise Paper 1 content
- after Paper 1 is completed, automatically shift focus to Paper 2 or next relevant paper
- deprioritise subjects already written
- show non-exam days as recovery or catch-up planning days where relevant

The official timetable includes non-examination days on 03/11, 04/11, 05/11, and 09/11, which can be used for catch-up or revision planning. :contentReference[oaicite:21]{index=21} :contentReference[oaicite:22]{index=22}

4. EXAM COUNTDOWN ENGINE
Build a countdown engine that:
- calculates days until each learner’s next exam
- shows urgency states:
  - 14+ days = build mastery
  - 7–13 days = focused revision
  - 3–6 days = exam prep mode
  - 0–2 days = final revision sprint
- updates automatically every day

6. SUBJECT-SPECIFIC STUDY PLAN LOGIC
The study planner must use:
- official exam date
- learner performance data
- weak topics
- paper type
- days remaining

Example behaviour:
- If Mathematics P1 is 5 days away, prioritise algebra, functions, calculus, and timed practice
- If Geography P2 is 2 days away, prioritise settlements, economic geography, and mapwork-linked revision
- If Life Sciences P1 is complete and P2 is next, shift to Paper 2 content automatically

It must dynamically populate revision content based on the learner’s chosen NSC subjects and the official exam sequence from the final timetable.
