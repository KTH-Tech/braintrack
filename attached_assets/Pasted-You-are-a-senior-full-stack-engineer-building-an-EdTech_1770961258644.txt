4) Adaptive Weekly Drill Generator

- Wed: 25 mark case burst (lowest mastery pillar)
- Fri: 40 mark timed mixed
- Sun: 80 mark mini mock (20 MCQ + 2 cases)
Mastery thresholds:
- red < 60%
- amber 60–75%
- green 75–85%
- mastery+ >= 85%

- If topic_mastery < 60%: increase sampling +40%, easier difficulty
- If 60–75%: +20% sampling, medium difficulty, add more cases
